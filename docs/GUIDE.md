# Guide for Elizabeth, her family and carers

## What the app does

The app speaks for you. Choose a phrase, or type, and press **SPEAK**. Everything happens
on this tablet: nothing you say, type or record is sent anywhere. The app is not able to
use the internet at all.

## Using it

- **Yes, No, Wait, Help and Thank you** are always on the left. One press says them
  straight away.
- **Phrases** are grouped in tabs along the top: Needs, Feelings, Chat, Care, Questions.
  Press a phrase to add it to your message. Use **◀ Back** and **Next ▶** for more
  phrases.
- **ABC** opens the keyboard. Word suggestions appear in the row under the message: press
  one to save typing. Messages you have said before are offered too.
- **Recent** shows what you have said lately.
- **SPEAK** says the message. **Say again** repeats the last thing you said.
- **Undo** reverses your last press. **Delete word** and **Clear** remove text.
- **🔔 Call** plays a chime to call someone, even if speech isn't working.
- If speech ever fails, your message is shown in large letters for people to read.

## Pressing buttons

You don't need to be accurate:

- A touch just outside a button counts for the nearest one.
- You can land on the wrong button and slide your finger to the right one before lifting.
  The button with the yellow border is the one that will be pressed.
- Brief brushes, slips and accidental double presses are ignored.

Carers can change all of this in Settings → Touch, for example to press by resting your
finger on a button instead.

## For carers

- **Settings:** hold the ⚙ Settings button for 2 seconds. A PIN can be added. After 5
  wrong PINs, entry is locked for a while.
- **Phrases and recordings:** add or change phrases, and record her saying them. Recorded
  phrases play in her real voice. Do this early, while her voice is strong.
- **Voice banking:** she reads sentences so a synthetic voice can be made from her voice.
  Short sessions are fine. See [tools/voice-training](../tools/voice-training/README.md)
  for how the voice is made.
- **Touch suggestions:** if switched on, the app counts how touches go (numbers only) and
  suggests changes. You decide whether to apply them.
- **Switch scanning:** for when touch becomes too hard. It works with one or two
  accessibility switches.
- **Keyguard:** saves a cutting template for an acrylic keyguard that matches the screen.
- **Always available:** make the app the tablet's home screen, so it comes back after a
  restart or crash.
- **Paper board:** print a paper back-up of her phrases and the alphabet. Print it again
  whenever the phrases change.
- **Privacy and data:** see what the app knows, switch learning or history off, export a
  backup, restore, or erase.
- **Back-ups:** export regularly (Settings → Privacy and data → Export all data). Keep the
  file and passphrase safe. Without the passphrase nobody can open it, including you.

## About the AI in this app (please read)

- **Her voice model is AI-generated.** It is made from her recordings. It will sound like
  her, but not perfectly, and it may occasionally mispronounce names or unusual words. Add
  those words as recorded phrases, or spell them differently.
- **Word prediction is simple statistics** of the words she uses. It only suggests; she
  always chooses what is said. It can be switched off, and single words can be forgotten.
- **Nothing is sent anywhere.** All of this runs on the tablet.
- **The cloned voice is only for her own communication.** Speech is played aloud, never
  saved as audio files. It is good manners to tell new people that it is a computer voice
  made from her own. There is a ready-made phrase for this in Chat.
- **Withdrawing consent:** she (or a donor) can withdraw consent at any time. This deletes
  the recordings and any voice made from them on this tablet. Copies that were exported
  must be deleted separately.

## If something goes wrong

- **No sound:** check the volume. A warning appears at the top when it is low.
- **The chosen voice won't load:** the tablet's built-in voice is used instead, and a
  message says so.
- **The tablet is flat or broken:** use the paper board.
